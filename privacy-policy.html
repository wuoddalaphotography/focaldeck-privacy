FocalDeck Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026
Developer: WuodDala Photography
Contact Email: wuoddalaphotography01@gmail.com
Android Package: com.focaldeck.studio

1. Overview

FocalDeck ("the App") is a 3D optical simulation, dual-rig lens comparison, and lighting pre-visualization app. Its core 3D studio, optical physics calculations, and local PNG/EXIF exports operate offline on your device without requiring an account.

2. Information Processed by the App

A. Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA)

Camera access is used only when you open Scan Scene or Scan Gear. On-device exposure metering runs locally. If you initiate AI Scene Analysis or AI Gear Recognition, the captured image or short clip is sent over encrypted HTTPS to the configured AI backend (or directly to Google's Gemini API when using Bring-Your-Own-Key) solely to return coaching tips or detected equipment details.

B. Optional AI Photoreal Previews & Safety Reports

  • Render AI: When you tap Render AI, the current 3D comparison preview frame and optical rig parameters (focal length, aperture, shutter, ISO, lighting angles) are sent over HTTPS to generate the requested preview image. Cached renders stay in private local storage and can be cleared anytime in Settings → Clear AI Render Cache.
  • AI Safety Reports: When you submit a report via Report AI Generated Image, your feedback note, render state hash, AI model name, and app version are transmitted over HTTPS to help monitor safety and quality.

C. Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) Credentials

Optional Gemini API keys entered in Settings are encrypted on-device using hardware-backed AES-256-GCM via AndroidKeyStore. They are never sent to FocalDeck servers and are transmitted only directly to generativelanguage.googleapis.com when you run an AI action.

D. Device Integrity & Google Play Billing

  • Firebase App Check / Play Integrity: Used to verify app authenticity and protect cloud AI endpoints from abuse.
  • Google Play Billing: Subscriptions (focaldeck_pro_monthly) and credit packs (ai_credits_25) are processed directly by Google Play. FocalDeck never accesses or stores your payment card details.

E. Local Storage & Exports

Your default camera/lens rig, gear inventory, and preferences are stored privately on your device. PNG comparisons are written to Pictures/FocalDeck only when you choose to save or share.

3. Data Sharing & Advertising

FocalDeck includes no third-party advertising SDKs, does not sell personal data, and does not build advertising profiles.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

FocalDeck requires no account registration. You can clear local caches or stored BYOK keys in Settings or uninstall the App to remove all local data. For inquiries regarding submitted AI safety reports, email wuoddalaphotography01@gmail.com.

5. Children's Privacy

FocalDeck is intended for a general photography audience and is not directed at children under 13 years of age.

6. Updates

Any updates to this Privacy Policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
